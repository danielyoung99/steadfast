Accessibility statement

Draft: needs a legal check

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA.

What we have done

  • Every page works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Headings and landmarks are in order, with a skip link on each page.
  • Colours follow your light or dark setting.
  • Animation is off if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Form fields have labels and errors are announced.
